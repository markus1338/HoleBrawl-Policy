Hole Brawl – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Hole Brawl does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app works without an internet connection.

Your player name, chosen country flag and game settings are stored only locally on your device. We cannot access them. Deleting the app removes them.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses (see the GitHub Privacy Statement). We use no cookies or tracking here.

If a future version processes personal data, this policy will be updated before release.
