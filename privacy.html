Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

In short: Hole Brawl does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app works completely offline.

1. Controller

Responsible for data processing in connection with the Hole Brawl app and this website:

Markus Hassler
E-mail: info@hassler-solutions.ch

This policy is based on the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Scope

This policy applies to the iOS app "Hole Brawl" (the "app") and to this website. It explains which personal data is processed, for what purpose and what rights you have.

3. The app

3.1 No collection of personal data

  • We do not collect, store or receive any personal data through the app.
  • The app does not require an account or a login.
  • The app contains no advertising and no in-app purchases.
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting, attribution or advertising services, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites and do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • The app does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • The app works without an internet connection and does not send data to us or to third parties.

3.2 Data stored locally on your device

To provide the game, the app stores the following data exclusively on your device: the player name you enter, the country flag you choose, your game and sound settings and your selected skills. This data never leaves your device and we have no access to it. You can change it at any time in the settings. Deleting the app removes it completely.

The player name is displayed only on your own device (for example in the leaderboard after a round). All opponents in the game are computer-controlled; no data is exchanged with other players.

3.3 Apple App Store

The app is distributed through Apple's App Store. When you download the app, Apple processes data (for example your Apple Account and purchase history) under its own responsibility and privacy policy. We do not receive personal data about you from Apple. Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous statistics (for example the number of downloads), which do not allow us to identify you.

3.4 Device features

The app may use vibration (haptic feedback) on your device. This does not involve any processing of personal data.

4. This website

4.1 Hosting

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the website, GitHub processes technical access data, in particular your IP address, the date and time of access, the requested page and browser information, in order to deliver the website and ensure its security and stability. We have no access to these server logs. Data may be transferred to the USA; GitHub relies on recognised transfer mechanisms for this. Details: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

4.2 No cookies, no tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics tools, social media plugins or tracking technologies of any kind.

4.3 Contact by e-mail

If you contact us by e-mail, we process your e-mail address and the content of your message solely to handle your request. We delete this data once your request has been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply. Legal basis under the GDPR: Art. 6(1)(b) and (f).

5. Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Since the app does not collect personal data from anyone, it also does not collect data from children.

6. Data security

Because the app does not transmit personal data, no personal data is stored on our systems. This website is delivered exclusively via an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

7. Your rights

Under the FADP and, where applicable, the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to withdraw any consent given. Since we do not process personal data through the app, we usually hold no data about you. To exercise your rights, contact us at info@hassler-solutions.ch.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority: in Switzerland, the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC, www.edoeb.admin.ch); in the EU, the supervisory authority of your country of residence.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the app gains new features. If a future version processes personal data (for example online play), we will update this policy and the privacy information in the App Store before that version is released. The current version is always available on this page.
