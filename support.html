FAQ

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. Hole Brawl works completely offline and without an account.

Who are my opponents?

All opponents are computer-controlled holes with their own play styles.

How do I steer?

Drag your finger anywhere on the screen. In Skill Mode, your two skills appear as buttons on the side of the screen; you can move them in the settings.

What is the difference between Classic and Skill Mode?

Classic Arena is pure swallowing action. In Skill Mode you pick two of five skills before each round – Dash, Parry, Mass Split, Overload and Leech. Every skill costs mass, so timing matters.

Why was I eliminated while the app was in the background?

A round keeps running. If you leave the app for more than 10 seconds during a round, your hole is out.

How do I reset my name and settings?

Change them in the settings, or delete and reinstall the app.

Does Hole Brawl collect my data?

No. See our Privacy Policy.
